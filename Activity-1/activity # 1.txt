1. Activity Scenario: Can We Trust Our Data? 
You have joined a data-science team working for a government analytics organization. The organization wants to  develop a system that can identify individuals who are likely to have an annual income above $50,000. The  system may eventually be used to understand income patterns, design economic programs, identify groups  requiring financial support, and perform socioeconomic analysis. 
You have been given the UCI Adult Income dataset collected from census data. However, the data is not ready for  analysis. Your responsibility is NOT simply to train a machine-learning model. Your responsibility is to  determine whether the data can be trusted for downstream analysis. 
2. Dataset 
Use the UCI Adult dataset: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/2/adult 
The dataset contains demographic and employment-related attributes and a target indicating whether annual  income is above or below $50K. 
3. Rules of the Activity 
• Do not begin by blindly applying a standard preprocessing recipe. 
• First investigate the dataset and identify problems. 
• Every important cleaning decision must be supported by evidence. 
• For each decision, record: Problem → Evidence → Action → Justification. 
• Distinguish an unusual observation from an erroneous observation. 
• Be alert to data leakage when preparing data for downstream machine learning. 
• Your notebook should be reproducible and clearly documented.
